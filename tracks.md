"Wing" means Front/Rear Wing Aero (0–50): low wing = straight-line speed, high wing = corner grip.

## 1. Bahrain — Bahrain International Circuit (Sakhir)

- **Type:** Permanent desert track, abrasive. Straights combined with slow-medium corners.
- **Key zones:** T1 (downhill braking), T9–T10 (fast-medium combo), T14 (traction onto start/finish straight).
- **Tyres:** Rear-limited, high degradation. Rear tyres overheat quickly on traction out of slow corners.
- **Brakes/traction:** Heavy braking + traction priority. Avoid an overly locked diff to preserve the rear tyres.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Relatively smooth, medium-low ride height works. Avoid going too stiff or traction becomes edgy.
- **Setup implication:** Medium wing. Do not run maximum tyre pressures (heat). Slightly forward brake bias for T1 stability.
- **Race:** Easy overtaking (T1, T4, T11). Strong undercut. Desert wind changes balance.

## 2. Saudi Arabia — Jeddah Corniche Circuit

- **Type:** Fastest street circuit. Close walls, consecutive high-speed Esses.
- **Key zones:** S1 Esses (high speed), T13 (contrasting slow hairpin), back-straight DRS.
- **Tyres:** High lateral load at high speed, front tyres work hard. Medium deg.
- **Brakes/traction:** Medium braking, but high-speed commitment matters more than late braking.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Smooth, can run stiff with low ride height for aero stability. Minimal kerb use.
- **Setup implication:** Medium-low wing for top speed without making S1 nervous. Fairly firm front ARB for responsiveness.
- **Race:** Frequent Safety Car/VSC (close walls). Qualifying matters even with DRS.

## 3. Australia — Albert Park (Melbourne)

- **Type:** Semi-street park circuit, reprofiled layout is faster and more flowing.
- **Key zones:** T3 (heavy braking), T9–T10 (fast chicane), T11–T13 (final combo).
- **Tyres:** Medium front stress. Large track evolution as grip builds through the weekend.
- **Brakes/traction:** Medium-heavy braking, traction out of the chicanes matters.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Slightly bumpy, avoid going too stiff. Medium ride height to prevent bottoming at T9–T10.
- **Setup implication:** Medium wing. Chase efficiency: enough wing for S2 without killing the start/finish straight.
- **Race:** Medium overtaking (T3, T11). SC/red flag possible. Practice matters for reading grip.

## 4. Azerbaijan — Baku City Circuit

- **Type:** Extreme contrast: 2.2 km straight plus narrow slow castle section.
- **Key zones:** T1–T3 (90-degree braking), T8–T11 castle (narrow), T16 onto the straight (traction defines top speed).
- **Tyres:** Rear traction + brake temperatures. Low deg on the straight, but easy lock-up at T1/T3.
- **Brakes/traction:** Braking priority. T16 traction is critical to overtake or avoid being passed on the straight.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Bumpy public roads, sharp castle kerbs. Do not run too stiff, use medium ride height for kerb safety.
- **Setup implication:** Low-medium wing. Do not use 100% brake pressure (easy lock-up). Medium on-throttle diff for clean T16 traction.
- **Race:** Safety Car almost guaranteed. Easy overtaking at T1/T3 with DRS. Qualifying + SC luck decide a lot.

## 5. USA — Miami International Autodrome

- **Type:** Temporary street circuit around the stadium. Low-grip slippery asphalt plus heat.
- **Key zones:** Back-straight DRS, technical narrow T11–T16 complex, T17 hairpin.
- **Tyres:** Slippery rear, easy to spin. Fast overheating in hot conditions.
- **Brakes/traction:** Traction + rear stability priority. A more open diff helps.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Bumps in places, aggressive kerbs at the overpass chicane. Medium ride height.
- **Setup implication:** Medium wing. Do not run the rear suspension too stiff. Avoid maximum rear tyre pressures.
- **Race:** Medium overtaking (DRS at T11/T17). SC possible. Manage tyres over long stints.

## 6. Emilia Romagna — Imola

- **Type:** Old-school, narrow, flowing, elevation changes. Punishes small mistakes.
- **Key zones:** Tamburello–Villeneuve (fast), Acque Minerali (down-uphill), Rivazza (final traction).
- **Tyres:** Medium. Balanced lateral load front to rear.
- **Brakes/traction:** Fast direction changes + downhill braking. Stability under load transfer matters.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Tall kerbs with grass/gravel outside. Needs compliance: medium suspension, not ultra-low ride height, to avoid being thrown by kerbs.
- **Setup implication:** Medium-high wing. Balanced ARB, not too firm, so the car is not nervous through chicanes.
- **Race:** Very hard to overtake. Qualifying + undercut strategy. SC rare but costly.

## 7. Monaco — Monte Carlo

- **Type:** Slowest and narrowest street track. No meaningful straight.
- **Key zones:** Loews Hairpin (slowest), Portier + tunnel, Nouvelle Chicane (only heavy braking), Rascasse.
- **Tyres:** Low deg, but graining if there is too much sliding. Temperatures hard to maintain.
- **Brakes/traction:** Low-speed traction + rotation is everything. Front lock-up is easy (slippery surface).
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Needs compliance: soft suspension, higher ride height for bumps/kerbs and road camber. Maximum steering lock.
- **Setup implication:** Maximum wing. Slightly more rearward brake bias than other tracks to avoid front lock-up in slow corners. Open diff for rotation.
- **Race:** Almost impossible to overtake. Qualifying equals the race. Frequent SC but small pit loss. Usually one stop.

## 8. Spain — Barcelona-Catalunya

- **Type:** True aero test. Long medium-high-speed corners.
- **Key zones:** T3 (long right), T9 (fast blind), technical slow S3.
- **Tyres:** Classic front-limited track, front-left punished. High deg.
- **Brakes/traction:** Medium. More about minimum speed through long corners.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Smooth, can run stiff with low ride height for a stable aero platform.
- **Setup implication:** Medium-high wing. Avoid extreme front camber that kills straights and wears the inside edge. Medium front pressures.
- **Race:** Hard to overtake despite DRS (T1). Deg decides one vs. two stops.

## 9. Canada — Montreal (Gilles Villeneuve)

- **Type:** Fast semi-street track, close walls, low grip. Braking plus top speed.
- **Key zones:** T10 hairpin (traction), final chicane + Wall of Champions (do not touch the wall).
- **Tyres:** Rear traction + hot brakes. Medium deg.
- **Brakes/traction:** Repeated heavy braking. High brake pressure is risky because of bumps.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Large sausage kerbs at the chicanes. Compliant suspension plus medium-high ride height to cut kerbs without launching the car.
- **Setup implication:** Low-medium wing. Forward brake bias for stability. Medium-open on-throttle diff for clean hairpin traction.
- **Race:** Good overtaking (hairpin, final chicane). Very frequent SC. Brake blistering plus variable weather.

## 10. Austria — Red Bull Ring

- **Type:** Short, elevation changes, only 10 corners. Lap times are tight, so small qualifying errors are costly.
- **Key zones:** T1 (uphill braking), T3 (sharp downhill braking), T9–T10 (fast run to the line).
- **Tyres:** Medium. Traction out of slow uphill corners.
- **Brakes/traction:** Uphill/downhill traction + braking. Rear stability on initial acceleration matters.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Kerbs plus sausage kerbs outside, strict track limits in-game. Medium ride height, not too low.
- **Setup implication:** Medium wing. Balance: enough wing for S2–S3 without being slow across 3 DRS zones.
- **Race:** Easy overtaking with 3 DRS zones. Sprint format in the real calendar. Traffic plus track limits decide results.

## 11. Great Britain — Silverstone

- **Type:** Fastest flowing track: Maggotts-Becketts-Chapel, Copse, Stowe.
- **Key zones:** Copse (commitment), Maggotts/Becketts (direction change), Vale/Club (final traction).
- **Tyres:** Very high front lateral load at high speed. Medium-high deg.
- **Brakes/traction:** Minimum speed through fast corners matters more than late braking.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Smooth and fast, can run stiff for aero platform. Copse/Stowe kerbs partly usable.
- **Setup implication:** High but efficient wing (not maximum). Firm suspension/ARB for fast-S response. Enough front camber for lateral grip.
- **Race:** Medium-good overtaking (Stowe, Vale). Crosswind changes Copse/Maggotts. Medium SC chance.

## 12. Hungary — Hungaroring

- **Type:** "Monaco without walls": relentless twisty slow-medium corners.
- **Key zones:** Uphill T2–T5 chicane, T11 hairpin, T13–T14 traction onto the straight.
- **Tyres:** Hot plus high deg. Tyres get no rest because there is no cooling straight.
- **Brakes/traction:** Slow-exit traction plus momentum conservation. Easy rear overheating.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Medium kerbs, dusty off-line. Medium ride height.
- **Setup implication:** High wing. Do not run too locked a diff (wear + overheating). Brake/tyre cooling priority.
- **Race:** Very hard to overtake. Qualifying + undercut. One-two stops depending on heat.

## 13. Belgium — Spa-Francorchamps

- **Type:** Longest track, big elevation, micro-weather. Legendary Eau Rouge/Raidillon.
- **Key zones:** La Source (traction toward Eau Rouge), Kemmel (slipstream), double medium-speed Pouhon, flat Blanchimont.
- **Tyres:** Medium-high. Eau Rouge compression plus Blanchimont load.
- **Brakes/traction:** Medium braking (La Source, Les Combes, Bus Stop). Straight efficiency vs. S2 grip.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Eau Rouge needs a stable yet compliant platform under compression. Do not run too low (bottoming).
- **Setup implication:** Classic low-medium wing. Too low is wild at Pouhon/Blanchimont; too high means getting passed on Kemmel.
- **Race:** Easy overtaking on Kemmel. Wet in one sector and dry in another is normal. SC/red flag possible.

## 14. Netherlands — Zandvoort

- **Type:** Short, banked corners (T3, T14), sand outside.
- **Key zones:** Banked T3 (momentum), fast S2 sequence, banked Arie Luyendijk onto the line (traction + DRS).
- **Tyres:** Front works hard in banking plus fast corners. Medium deg.
- **Brakes/traction:** Banked-exit traction plus minimum-speed maintenance.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Banking compresses the suspension, gravel punishes mistakes. Medium ride height, not too stiff.
- **Setup implication:** High wing. Downforce gives confidence in the banking.
- **Race:** Very hard to overtake (mainly T1). Qualifying + banked start. Rare SC.

## 15. Italy — Monza (Temple of Speed)

- **Type:** Lowest-wing race of the year. Four straights plus hard chicanes plus Parabolica.
- **Key zones:** T1 (heavy braking from 350 to 80), Roggia, Ascari (kerbs), Parabolica (traction onto the straight).
- **Tyres:** Low-medium. Brakes suffer far more than tyres.
- **Brakes/traction:** Extreme braking plus Parabolica traction defines start/finish straight top speed.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Cutting T1/Roggia/Ascari kerbs equals lap time. Needs compliance plus not ultra-low ride height to avoid bouncing off kerbs.
- **Setup implication:** Minimum wing. Stable forward brake bias, do not overdo pressure. Soft-medium suspension despite straight dominance.
- **Race:** Very strong slipstream + DRS. Easy overtaking at T1. Frequent SC.

## 16. Singapore — Marina Bay (2023 layout)

- **Type:** Bumpiest, hottest, longest street track. 2023 layout: T16–T19 chicane removed, longer run into T16.
- **Key zones:** T1–T3 (early braking), Anderson Bridge, old technical S3, new straight into T16 braking.
- **Tyres:** Rear plus brakes suffer badly. Humidity plus no cooling.
- **Brakes/traction:** Repeated braking plus traction out of 90-degree corners. Lock-up and brake overheating are normal.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Very bumpy plus street kerbs. Soft suspension, high ride height. Stiff destroys tyres and slides.
- **Setup implication:** High/maximum wing. Brake cooling priority. Open diff for traction + tyre life.
- **Race:** Safety Car almost guaranteed (sometimes multiple). Flexible strategy, small pit loss. Focus on survival plus brake management.

## 17. Japan — Suzuka

- **Type:** Figure-8 driver favorite. Precision plus high-speed bravery.
- **Key zones:** S-Curves (flow), Degner (precision), Spoon (momentum + traction), 130R (flat), Casio Triangle (braking).
- **Tyres:** Very high front lateral load in S1. Medium-high deg.
- **Brakes/traction:** One significant heavy braking zone (Casio). The rest is minimum speed.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Smooth, fast direction changes need response. Can run firm plus low-medium ride height.
- **Setup implication:** High wing. A slightly safe understeer balance is faster than a nervous oversteer car in S1.
- **Race:** Medium overtaking (T1, Casio, 130R if brave). Typhoon/weather possible. S1 deg defines stints.

## 18. Qatar — Lusail International Circuit

- **Type:** Fast flowing, flat, smooth. Almost no slow corners.
- **Key zones:** Fast T4–T5 combo, T6 hairpin (only heavy braking), flowing S3.
- **Tyres:** Sustained lateral load on the front-left. High deg despite smooth surface.
- **Brakes/traction:** Light braking except T6. Minimum speed plus high-speed stability.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Smooth, cool night conditions ideal. Can run stiff and low for aero.
- **Setup implication:** Medium-high wing. Protect the front tyres: do not run aggressive camber/pressures.
- **Race:** Medium overtaking (T1/T6 plus straight). Track limits plus desert wind. Night means consistent grip.

## 19. USA — COTA (Austin)

- **Type:** Bit of everything: Suzuka-style Esses plus hairpin plus straight plus slow stadium.
- **Key zones:** Blind uphill T1 (braking), S1 Esses, T11 hairpin into DRS, T13–T15 stadium.
- **Tyres:** Balanced front (Esses) plus rear (hairpin/stadium traction).
- **Brakes/traction:** Heavy at T1/T11/T12. Hairpin-exit traction defines the back-straight overtake.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Famous bumps plus wind. Medium ride height, not too stiff a suspension for T1 and Esses stability.
- **Setup implication:** Classic medium wing. Compromise: enough wing for Esses/stadium but not dead on the back straight.
- **Race:** Good overtaking (T1, T11–T12). Wind + bumps change balance every lap. One-two stop strategy.

## 20. Mexico — Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez

- **Type:** 2,285 m altitude: thin air. Low drag plus low effective downforce plus poor cooling.
- **Key zones:** T1 (braking), S-Complex, slow Foro Sol stadium, 1.2 km straight.
- **Tyres:** Low deg (low grip), but easy sliding. Brakes + PU run hot.
- **Brakes/traction:** Braking feels longer (thin air). Stadium plus T1 traction.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Smooth, stadium kerbs usable. Medium-low ride height.
- **Setup implication:** High/maximum wing number to compensate for thin air, but it still feels like low downforce. Cooling plus stable forward brake bias.
- **Race:** Very strong slipstream on the straight. Easy overtaking at T1. Start + SC decide it.

## 21. Brazil — Interlagos (Jose Carlos Pace)

- **Type:** Short, anti-clockwise, elevation, unpredictable weather. Flowing plus atmosphere.
- **Key zones:** Senna S (downhill braking), uphill T4, Juncao (traction onto uphill finish), T1.
- **Tyres:** Medium. Neck load to the right (anti-clockwise) plus uphill traction.
- **Brakes/traction:** Juncao plus T2 traction defines uphill start/finish speed and DRS.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Bumps plus aggressive inside kerbs. Compliant suspension plus medium ride height.
- **Setup implication:** Medium-high wing. No need for Monza wing, but S2 needs grip.
- **Race:** Very good overtaking (Senna S, T1, T4). Rain at any time. Sprint + frequent SC. Momentum plus wet/dry strategy.

## 22. USA — Las Vegas Strip Circuit (new)

- **Type:** Super-fast cold night street track. About 2 km Strip straight plus slow 90-degree corners.
- **Key zones:** Early T1–T4 combo, Sphere, Strip straight (DRS), heavy T14 braking at the end of the straight.
- **Tyres:** Low grip plus cold. Hard to put heat into the tyres, possible front graining.
- **Brakes/traction:** Cold brakes equal easy lock-up at T1/T14. Traction out of slow corners onto straights.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Smooth-undulating public roads plus slippery white lines. Medium ride height (high-speed bumps).
- **Setup implication:** Minimum-low wing. Do not run too-low tyre pressures so tyres warm up faster. Conservative brake pressure.
- **Race:** Easy overtaking on the Strip/T14. SC very likely (walls + cold). Tyre warm-up plus fuel load decide pace.

## 23. Abu Dhabi — Yas Marina Circuit

- **Type:** Smooth twilight track: fast S1 straights plus slow technical S3 under the hotel.
- **Key zones:** T5–T7 braking chicane, dual back-straight DRS, slow T13–T16 S3.
- **Tyres:** Medium rear (S3 traction). Medium deg, temperatures fall at night.
- **Brakes/traction:** S3 traction plus chicane braking. Slow rotation matters.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Very smooth, low kerbs. Can run low plus fairly stiff for S3 response.
- **Setup implication:** Medium wing. Compromise: not too low or S3 dies, not too high or rivals pass on the 2 straights.
- **Race:** Medium-good overtaking (T5, T9). Large grip evolution as temperatures drop. Strong undercut.

## 24. China — Shanghai International Circuit (bonus)

- **Type:** Legendary front-limited track. Long T1–T2 spiral plus snail hairpin plus 1.2 km straight.
- **Key zones:** Long T1–T4 spiral (kills front tyres), snail T6 hairpin (traction), T14 onto the straight (overtake).
- **Tyres:** Extreme front-left stress, graining. Front deg defines stints.
- **Brakes/traction:** Heavy braking at T6/T14. Snail plus T14 traction.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Wide and smooth, safe kerbs. Can run low and stable.
- **Setup implication:** Medium wing. Protect the front: conservative camber/pressures, slightly safe understeer balance lasts longer.
- **Race:** Easy overtaking at T14 with DRS. Front vs. rear strategy. Dust outside the racing line.

## 25. France — Paul Ricard (bonus)

- **Type:** Flat, smooth, giant blue-red run-off. Mistral straight plus chicane plus fast Signes.
- **Key zones:** Early T1–T2, Mistral chicane (braking), fast Signes (commitment), double Beausset (momentum).
- **Tyres:** Medium. High track temperatures plus Mistral wind.
- **Brakes/traction:** Medium braking (chicane). Minimum speed at Signes/Beausset.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Striped kerbs plus flat run-off. Can run stiff and low. Wind changes straight vs. corner balance.
- **Setup implication:** Medium wing. Efficiency: enough for Signes but fast on Mistral.
- **Race:** Medium overtaking (chicane, T1). Generous track limits due to run-off. Temperature + wind strategy.

## 26. Portugal — Portimao (Algarve, bonus)

- **Type:** Blind uphill-downhill rollercoaster. Fast blind corners plus downhill braking.
- **Key zones:** Blind downhill T1, fast right-hand T8–T10, downhill T14–T15 to the line, uphill-traction T5 hairpin.
- **Tyres:** Balanced lateral plus compression/elevation load. Medium deg.
- **Brakes/traction:** Downhill braking (easy lock-up) plus uphill traction.
- **Suspension/kerbs:** Blind crests need compliance. Do not run too stiff/low or the car gets thrown over compressions and bottoms out.
- **Setup implication:** Medium-high wing. Downforce gives confidence in fast blind corners. Stable forward brake bias for descents.
- **Race:** Medium overtaking (T1, T5). Strong Atlantic wind changes braking. Qualifying + rhythm matter.

## How To Use This File

1. Check the track row in the summary table for wing direction and tyre stress.
2. Read the track section for the priority: traction vs. braking vs. fast corners.
3. Translate it into `AGENTS.md`: for example Monza means low wing plus forward brake bias plus compliant suspension for kerbs; Monaco/Singapore means high wing plus soft suspension plus safe ride height.
